Game data on your device

The app stores player names, scores, unfinished games, match history, statistics and preferences locally on your device.

Earlier Android releases may send selected game-usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase; these events do not deliberately include player names or complete match records. Android version 2.09 uses the existing Google privacy message for measurement choices; it does not display a second Firebase consent screen.

You can remove local data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. Backup and restoration may also depend on your device settings and backup provider.

Optional usage statistics

Google Analytics for Firebase follows the analytics-storage signal from the existing Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy message. Collection stays blocked until the message has resolved and a valid applicable signal permits it. Eligibility to show ads alone is not permission for Analytics. You can play without statistics and change the relevant choices from Privacy and diagnostics, which reopens that same privacy message.

Our instrumented events describe the displayed screen (lobby, game, statistics or privacy), a game starting, completing or being abandoned, the game type, individual or team mode, whether the game was resumed, and a fixed reason for abandonment. We do not add player names or IDs, scores, player counts, game duration, bot level, purchase tokens or custom purchase events.

The SDK also collects automatic events and technical information, including installation/app-instance and session identifiers, app lifecycle and engagement, device and app information, and approximate location derived from network information. Depending on SDK integrations, automatic purchase or advertising events may also occur. These data are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. We disable automatic native-screen reporting, Analytics advertising-ID collection and Analytics advertising personalization. Advertising services have their own separate consent flow.

Collection is blocked until permission. On withdrawal, we block app events, disable collection, deny Analytics storage and reset local Analytics data. This does not recall uploads in progress or erase records Google already received. Earlier releases used broader usage and Premium events; previously collected data remain subject to service retention settings and legal obligations.

As configured on 15 November 2026, Google Analytics user-level and event-level data retention is set to 2 months, with reset on new activity disabled. Changes can take 24 hours to apply; eligible records are removed through the service’s monthly deletion process. This setting does not set a two-month lifetime for standard aggregated reports or for data held by advertising, payment or other services.

We rely on consent for optional statistics where applicable. Contact us about retention, access or deletion of already received records. An unresolved or unconfigured signal does not enable collection. Where UMP determines European consent requirements do not apply, measurement follows the configured regional defaults and applicable law; the app does not display an extra Firebase consent request.

Optional Android crash reporting

Firebase Crashlytics sends technical reports to Google on our behalf to diagnose and fix faults. For new consent choices, the existing Google privacy message obtains the publisher’s consent for storing/accessing device information and developing/improving the service where European consent requirements apply. Reports remain blocked without those applicable permissions. Users whose earlier consent does not contain these publisher purposes do not upload reports unless they provide the required permissions through the existing Privacy and diagnostics options. Outside that scope, the configured regional defaults apply. Automatic SDK upload remains off; the app explicitly sends eligible reports only after resolving these signals. There is no additional Firebase consent screen.

Reports can contain the exception and stack trace, technical application state, app and operating-system versions, device model, and installation identifiers used to distinguish affected installations. The Firebase Installations and Sessions libraries support crash reporting and app-quality diagnostics. This information is pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. We do not deliberately attach player names, emails, saved matches, purchase tokens or account identifiers to Crashlytics reports.

If the privacy message also permits usage statistics, crash reports may contain recent Analytics events (breadcrumbs). Analytics is not required for crash reporting. A change to either effective measurement permission discards pending reports, including reports from the running session at its next restart; reporting resumes for later sessions with unchanged choices.

Firebase SDK initialization may still involve basic configuration or service requests while optional collection is off. The optional choices control Analytics collection and crash-report upload; they do not promise that the app makes no network connections.

Reports may be stored on the device while upload is disabled. With your permission, eligible cached reports are sent on a subsequent app launch. When you newly enable reporting, the app requests deletion of previously unsent reports rather than uploading them as part of that new choice. You can withdraw applicable permission by opening Privacy and diagnostics and changing the choices in the existing Google privacy message. Disabling requests deletion of unsent reports and stops further app-initiated uploads; it cannot recall an upload already in progress or erase reports Google already received.

We use your consent for this optional reporting. Google processes the reports as our service provider. Crashlytics transport is encrypted. Google states that crash traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Contact randaczechstudio@gmail.com for privacy requests; do not send passwords or purchase tokens.

In Android version 2.09, Analytics and crash reporting follow their respective signals from the same privacy message; they are blocked until those signals have been resolved. We have not added Firebase Performance Monitoring or Google Ads integration for crash reporting. Separate advertising services remain covered by the advertising section. This section does not describe an iOS Crashlytics integration.

Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and mediation adapters for Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle). Partner participation depends on the advertising configuration.

Advertising services may process IP addresses, approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Purposes include delivering and measuring advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and security.

The app’s web content also includes a Google AdSense script, which may contact Google when loaded.

Consent and your choices

Where required, advertising consent choices are presented through Google User Messaging Platform. Available privacy options depend on your region and the applicable consent requirements.

Declining personalised advertising does not stop all technical processing. Earlier Analytics-enabled releases have their own behavior; in Android version 2.09, Analytics and crash reporting use the existing privacy message for their respective permissions and do not open additional consent screens. Advertising consent controls and device advertising-ID settings should not be understood as a general Analytics opt-out.

Where processing requires consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Contact us if you need assistance with your privacy choices.

Premium purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase processed through Google Play Billing.

The app receives purchase information, including the product identifier, purchase status and purchase token, to verify ownership, acknowledge purchases and unlock Premium. It does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

Earlier Analytics-enabled releases may record Premium purchase and activation events. In Android version 2.09, Premium does not determine your statistics or crash-reporting permissions. Our own Analytics events do not include purchase or activation events; the SDK may generate automatic events as described above.

Voice announcements

Voice announcements use operating-system text-to-speech services. This feature does not record your voice.

Processing by a text-to-speech service depends on your selected provider and device settings.

Retention, deletion and security

Local game data remains on your device until overwritten or cleared. Clearing local data does not automatically delete information already transmitted to analytics, advertising or payment services.

Providers retain data according to the relevant service settings, processing purposes and legal obligations. Analytics retention settings are described above.

Google states that Firebase SDKs and Google Mobile Ads encrypt collected user data in transit. Device security and backup protection also depend on your operating system and settings.

The app does not offer user-account registration or a dedicated in-app mechanism for requesting deletion of remotely processed data. Privacy requests can be sent to our contact email.
