Privacy Policy for DartScore Pro

Effective date: 13 September 2026

DartScore Pro is a darts scoring application. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app and how third-party services used by the app handle data.

1. Information stored by DartScore Pro

DartScore Pro stores game-related information such as scores, players, unfinished games, settings and statistics locally on your device using browser or WebView storage. This information is used to provide app functionality and is not intentionally used to identify you personally.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of DartScore Pro may display advertising provided through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your location, consent choices and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information.

Where required, consent choices are requested before advertising-related processing takes place. You can change certain advertising and privacy choices through the controls made available by Google and your device settings.

3. In-app purchases and Google Play Billing

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. DartScore Pro receives information needed to determine whether a valid purchase exists, but does not receive your complete payment card or bank account details. Payment information is handled by Google under Google's own privacy terms.

4. Technical data and hosting

The app loads its online content from infrastructure hosted by Vercel. Vercel and other network providers may process standard technical information required to deliver the service, such as IP addresses, request metadata, timestamps, browser or device information and security logs.

5. Voice features

DartScore Pro may use text-to-speech functionality provided by Android or the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Processing of speech output may therefore depend on the selected device or operating-system service. DartScore Pro does not intentionally use voice output to build a personal profile about you.

6. Cookies and local storage

DartScore Pro uses local device storage to remember game state and app preferences. Third-party services such as advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

7. Data retention

Game data stored locally remains on your device until it is overwritten, cleared by the app or browser/WebView storage, or removed when the app data is deleted. Data processed by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies and legal obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another jurisdiction with applicable privacy rights, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. Because most DartScore Pro game data is stored locally on your device, you can remove it by clearing the app's stored data or uninstalling the app.

9. Children's privacy

DartScore Pro is a general-audience darts scoring tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising and third-party services may apply additional age-related protections according to their own policies and applicable law.

10. Third-party services

Third-party services used by DartScore Pro may include Google Play, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, Android system services and Vercel. Their processing of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, legal requirements or third-party services change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions about DartScore Pro, please use the developer contact information published on the DartScore Pro listing in Google Play.
