About DartScore Pro

DartScore Pro is a scoring companion for people who want to play darts without stopping after every visit to calculate totals, remember turn order or write results on paper.

The app is designed for casual games at home, practice sessions and local matches with friends. Players select a game mode, configure the match, add player names and then enter each score as the match progresses. The scoreboard keeps the current state of the game visible and updates it after every confirmed visit.

Supported ways to play

DartScore Pro includes classic X01 scoring and additional game modes such as Cricket, Around the Clock and Roulette variants. X01 supports several starting scores, match formats using legs and sets, configurable finishing rules and two ways to enter scores: individual dart hits or the total for a three-dart visit.

For multiplayer games, the app keeps player order and makes it clear whose turn is active. X01 can also be played in teams. Players who want to practise alone can use a computer opponent with selectable difficulty, allowing a short practice session without needing another person at the board.

Scoring without mental arithmetic

A darts scorer is useful because the arithmetic becomes more demanding as a leg develops. DartScore Pro subtracts valid scores, protects the match state from invalid entries and keeps the remaining score visible. In supported X01 configurations it also respects the selected finishing rule, such as Double Out or Triple Out.

The app is not intended to replace knowledge of the rules. Instead, it handles the repetitive bookkeeping so players can concentrate on aiming, throwing and deciding which target to choose next.

Match continuity and statistics

DartScore Pro can keep unfinished game state on the device, which makes it possible to return to a match after an interruption. Saved match information is also used for local statistics and recent-match views. These features are designed around the same purpose as the scoreboard itself: making everyday darts sessions easier to follow.

Free and Premium use

The Android version can include advertising in the free experience and offers a Premium option through Google Play. Premium functionality and availability may change as the app evolves. Purchases in the Android app are processed by Google Play, while the web version is primarily provided as the online app experience and information source.

Who DartScore Pro is for

The app is aimed at recreational darts players, families, groups of friends and anyone practising common darts formats. It can also be useful when a physical scoreboard is not available or when players prefer to keep a digital record of a match.

Learn the game modes

If you are new to darts or want a reminder of how a particular mode works, see the DartScore Pro playing and scoring guide. It explains the basic flow of X01, Cricket, Around the Clock and the score-entry options used in the app.
